The Concept

Bypass the small talk.
Ignite real sparks.

Dating became a swipe. We put it back in a room. Every space here is built for something a screen can't do: proximity, timing, a shared silence. Immersive rooms, live matchmaking and theme nights — made for people who'd rather meet someone than message them.

General Info

Museum day tickets are €10 (student) and €15 (regular). Event tickets are €20 (student) and €25 (regular).
We blend digital art with real-world interactions. On entry, you can opt into anonymous matchmaking games on interactive screens.
Zero tolerance for non-consensual behaviour. Events are staffed with trained awareness guides and open with a consent briefing.
